# Tilik Officer privacy policy
Tilik Officer uses workspace membership, assignments, location information, inspection notes, status updates and photos to help field teams find and document conditions at a landfill. Device identifiers help manage access and synchronization. Records may be stored locally, including while offline, and shared through Apple iCloud (CloudKit) with authorized workspace participants.

Location and camera access are controlled through device Settings. Reports submitted to a shared workspace may remain available to the team after the app is removed. Contact the workspace operator to request access changes, corrections or deletion of team records.

For privacy questions, [contact Tilik support](https://github.com/Mweh/tilik-app-support/issues/new). Request a private follow-up before sharing personal or operational information.
