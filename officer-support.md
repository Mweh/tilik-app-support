# Tilik Officer support
Tilik Officer helps field teams receive assignments, find inspection locations and report conditions to their operator. To join a workspace, ask the operator for an invitation. For an assignment or access problem, contact the operator who manages the site.

For technical help, [open a support request](https://github.com/Mweh/tilik-app-support/issues/new). Include the device model, app version and a description. Requests are public. Do not post photos, coordinates, workspace invitations or personal details.
