# Tilik Operator privacy policy
Updated November 19, 2026.

Tilik Operator uses site and location information, monitoring zones, drone observations, images, inspection assignments, and field reports to support landfill monitoring. The app stores setup information and preferences on the device. Location access supports map and site setup. You can manage location permissions in device Settings.

When a team uses collaboration, workspace records are stored using Apple iCloud (CloudKit) and shared with invited participants. Participants can access workspace information according to their role. Device identifiers and connection records support workspace membership and synchronization. External equipment and map or weather services may process information needed to provide the feature you use.

Workspace owners can manage access and delete their workspace in the app. Removing the app from a device does not by itself delete shared workspace data. Contact your workspace operator about access, corrections, or removal of team records.

For privacy questions, [contact Tilik support](https://github.com/Mweh/tilik-app-support/issues/new). Request a private follow-up before providing personal or operational information.
