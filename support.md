# Tilik Operator support
Tilik Operator helps landfill teams organize monitoring zones, review survey results, and coordinate field inspections.

For help with setup, drone connectivity, or workspace invitations, [open a support request](https://github.com/Mweh/tilik-app-support/issues/new). Include your app version, device model, and the steps that led to the issue. Requests are public. Please omit personal details, location coordinates, invitation links, photos, passwords, and private workspace data.

For help with Home Point setup or starting a survey, open Settings → Tutorial in the app.
